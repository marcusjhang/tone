# Free the reveal, monetize the palette

Build a free, no-login web-and-mini-app that turns one selfie into a face-anchored **best-versus-worst drape reveal** and a one-tap branded result card — then refuse to charge for it. That single mechanic is the entire growth engine: the offline version of exactly this reveal produced **>20 million TikTok views from two videos by one customer** at COCORY and **30M+ views for a single creator**, while Jamface's digital AR version of the same diagnosis hit **1 million users in about four months** ([매일경제](https://www.mk.co.kr/news/business/10989038); [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/posts/from-k-pop-to-tiktok-is-color-season-analysis-a-missed-opportunity-for-brands); [Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/179303)). The card is what fans out — a short type label plus a face-under-colors image posted to Instagram Stories and KakaoTalk rooms, where friends ask "what am I?" and become the next diagnoses. Launch in Korea, the only market where personal color is already a mass norm (**54.4% of shoppers** consider it when buying) and where a **zero-install rail exists** (Toss mini-apps, "install hurdle 0") with KakaoTalk as the native share substrate ([매일경제](https://www.mk.co.kr/news/business/10769569); [Velog](https://velog.io/)). The highest-leverage unshipped feature is MBTI's **compatibility layer** — color-tone "chemistry" between friends and couples — which converts one result into two users and has no productized competitor in the category. Monetize only after scale and only downstream of the reveal: cosmetics commerce and affiliate, retail embedding, and paid human review; **never paywall the type, palette, best/worst, or card**, because that is what kills the loop ([App Store: Dressika](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1536745174); [App Store: My Best Colors](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/my-best-colors/id1459421275)). The risks are real — the "season" itself is an unsettled convention, and selfie color error is large enough to flip a type — so the product must ship confidence and nearest-neighbor types, blame the pairing rather than the person, and gate launch on deep-skin equity ([Cha & Kim 2011](https://doi.org/10.15810/jic.2011.7.4.002); [Niakan 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39627966/)).

## The winning mechanic is the drape reveal, not a better classifier

Korean customers buy a bundle — a compound type label plus best and worst palettes and per-category prescriptions for clothing, makeup, hair, jewelry, and glasses — but what spreads across social platforms is narrower and more emotional: **the same face under the "right" versus the "wrong" color** ([Korean JoongAng Daily](https://koreajoongangdaily.joins.com/2022/12/17/business/industry/korea-personal-color-personal-color-analysis/20221217070018534.html); [COCORY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=shH2koFA99k)). This is the exact unit that went megaviral offline: an Australian customer's two COCORY TikToks exceeded 20 million combined views and left COCORY with **150+ waitlisted visitors per month**, and creator Tatum Schwerin's single drape video passed **30 million views** ([매일경제](https://www.mk.co.kr/news/business/10989038); [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/posts/from-k-pop-to-tiktok-is-color-season-analysis-a-missed-opportunity-for-brands)). Vendors write the shock into their own copy — right colors make a face "lively and vigorous," wrong colors make "skin texture rough and the transparency disappear" — and viewers are invited to adjudicate, which is what drives comments, duets, and reposts ([App Store: Colorver](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/colorlover-personal-color-test/id1516558813); [App Store: iColor](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1207719454)).

The reason to bet the company on this and not on classifier accuracy is that a marginally better model is invisible to users while the reveal is inherently a performance. Digital precedents confirm both the demand and the feasibility. Jamface's AR 퍼컬매칭 reached 1M users in roughly four months, and after the diagnosis launched, in-app cosmetics search rose **7×**, product saves **24×**, and YouTuber-review views **4×** ([Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/179303); [Edaily](https://www.edaily.co.kr/News/Read?newsId=02200886632199360)). On the technical side, the render is solved at consumer fidelity: on-device face meshes (MediaPipe 468-point landmarks; ARKit face tracking; ARCore Augmented Faces with **no specialized hardware**) support a face-anchored color strip or soft tint, and colorwise already ships a "Color Analysis Camera" that physically projects colored light from the screen and photographs the face's response — no AR SDK, no diffusion model ([Google ARCore](https://developers.google.com/ar/develop/augmented-faces); [colorwise forum](https://forum.colorwise.me/t/from-draping-to-digital-my-best-colors-latest-innovation-revealed/6266)). The open lane is specific: **no shipped tool combines a face-anchored drape render, an explicit best-versus-worst contrast, and a one-tap branded share card** ([GitHub: Catch My Tone](https://github.com/choiseyeong/catch-my-tone); [App Store: WhatColors](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/whatcolors-ai-face-try-on/id6478835459)).

The MVP should therefore be deliberately thin:

| MVP scope | In | Out (until after the loop spins) |
|---|---|---|
| Input | One guided selfie; natural-light guidance; optional known-white calibration object in frame | Live AR camera, multi-frame photometry, hardware companion |
| Core render | Same face under best palette vs. worst palette (2–4 colors each) | Wardrobe/closet, garment swaps, generative relighting |
| Output | 4/8-type label, confidence + second type, palette swatches | Full result-sheet categories, product catalog, prescriptions |
| Artifact | One-tap branded card: 9:16 Story, 4:5 feed, KakaoTalk link preview | Community feed, profiles, streak mechanics |
| Delivery | No-login web + Toss mini-app, Korean-first | Native iOS/Android install |

Ship the reveal, the label, the confidence, and the card. Everything else is a later layer.

## The loop turns one result into two users

The growth loop is a direct port of the MBTI playbook, which is the best-documented viral schema in Korea: Korea has led the world in MBTI search volume since 2018 — indexed at **100 versus Hong Kong's 17 and the Philippines' 16** — because it produces a short, shareable, **non-hierarchical** identity code, backed by a free no-login test, a flattering designed result card people screenshot, and a surrounding ecosystem of compatibility charts and communities ([ditoday](https://ditoday.com/%ED%95%9C%EA%B5%AD%EC%9D%B8%EC%9D%B4-mbti%EB%A5%BC-%EC%A2%8B%EC%95%84%ED%95%98%EB%8A%94-%EC%9D%B4%EC%9C%A0/)). Personal color is already framed by vendors and media as the "**뷰티 MBTI**," and it carries a richer artifact than a four-letter code — a face plus palette — while test links already circulate inside KakaoTalk rooms ([Edaily](https://www.edaily.co.kr/News/Read?newsId=02200886632199360); [Naver blog via notes](https://blog.naver.com/)). The loop is: free no-login diagnosis → instantly generated, branded, screenshot-ready card → posted to KakaoTalk and Instagram Stories → friends ask "what am I?" → new diagnoses. The card must render as a clean KakaoTalk link preview and a Story image, because the messenger is the share substrate, not just a channel.

The single most valuable fan-out feature is the **un-ported MBTI mechanic: compatibility and matching**. MBTI type pages carry a dedicated "다른 유형과의 궁합" (compatibility) section, and personal color has a natural analogue — color-tone chemistry between two people — for which demand is already visible (couples use the diagnosis as a date course; idol-vs-idol tone debates are a durable genre) but **no shipped Korean personal-color app has productized it** ([namu.wiki INTP](https://namu.wiki/w/INTP); [Naver blog via notes](https://blog.naver.com/); [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/posts/from-k-pop-to-tiktok-is-color-season-analysis-a-missed-opportunity-for-brands)). Build three fan-out mechanics on top of the base card. First, **friend compare**: invite a friend and generate a joint card showing both palettes side by side, which converts every diagnosis into two users. Second, **compatibility/chemistry**: a friend-or-couple "tone chemistry" score and a shared best-color set, packaged as a shareable pair card — this is the highest-whitespace growth move available. Third, **invite-to-unlock on secondary content only**: gate the full palette, the "worst colors" detail, or extra diagnostics behind a referral, mirroring COCON's proven currency loop where "귤" is earned by friend referral, voting, or purchase — but let the hero reveal stay free so top-of-funnel never stalls ([AI Times](https://www.aitimes.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=152702)).

The **platform/friction ladder** matters more in Korea than anywhere else. The order is web link (open inside KakaoTalk) < Toss/Kakao mini-app (no signup, no download) < native App Store/Play download (install + login + permissions). Toss explicitly markets mini-apps that run "without a separate signup or app download," developers describe the install hurdle as **"0,"** and Toss has removed the business-registration requirement to launch one; a personal-color diagnosis mini-app already exists on those rails ([Toss Apps-in-Toss](https://developers-apps-in-toss.toss.im/checklist/publicity); [Velog](https://velog.io/); [carat.im](https://carat.im/v2/miniapps/ma%3Apersonal_color_diagnosis)). Jamface and Colorver both scaled *despite* forcing a native download — Colorver's 160K MAU was measured **app-only, with no web** — so install is not fatal, but a zero-install version should spread faster, all else equal ([ZDNet Korea](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20230223102403)). The recommendation is to default to **web + Toss mini-app for the diagnosis and card**, and push native only for retention and purchase. For global spillover, use the identical web link with localized cards; the wave model is not a linear Korea→Japan→SEA relay but a **simultaneous 2023–2024 explosion in the West (TikTok/tourism) and China (K-pop)**, now in a 2024–2026 localization phase of Western studios and at-home kits, with Japan an independent older market being re-entered by Korean product ([ColorMe.style](https://colorme.style/personal-color-analysis/); [MMNine Lab](https://www.mmnine-lab.com/style-guides/the-complete-guide-to-asian-colour-analysis-korean-japanese-amp-chinese); [글로벌경제](https://www.getnews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=857411)).

## Phase the build: seed the reveal, fan it out, localize, then charge

| Phase | Rough window | Job to be done | Milestone that unlocks the next phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 — Seeding** | Weeks 0–6 | Ship the thin MVP; recruit 10–30 Korean and global personal-color creators; replicate the proven format (a person under changing fabrics, an authority narrating, viewers judging) and the travel hook ("the Seoul result without the flight") | MVP live as web + Toss mini-app; baseline share-through-rate per completed result |
| **1 — Viral loop** | Weeks 4–16 | Ship the branded card, friend compare, compatibility/chemistry, and invite-to-unlock on secondary content; instrument the loop end to end | **Organic (unpaid) share-driven new sessions** become the majority of new users; 1M-test trajectory as the external benchmark Jamface set in ~4 months ([Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/179303)) |
| **2 — Scale & localize** | Months 4–12 | Localize result cards and cross-walk taxonomy (Korean 4/8-type ↔ Western 12-season; Chinese 黄皮/白皮/黑皮); recalibrate palettes for deep and non-East-Asian skin; embed in retail/kiosk/Toss-style surfaces; ride K-pop and beauty-tourism acquisition | A second market contributes a material share of new users; retail/creator partnership pilots live |
| **3 — Monetize** | Months 9–24 | Layer commerce/affiliate, retail B2B embedding, paid human review, and premium diagnostics; consider ads | Revenue per 1,000 active users positive while organic share still drives acquisition |

Two sequencing rules govern this plan. First, **do not build retention or commerce features before the loop demonstrably spins**, because the category's reported numbers are cumulative, not active: Colorver's 160K MAU against 800K downloads implies a directional **~20% download-to-MAU ratio**, and Jamface's "1M in 4 months" is best read as a viral reach event, not sustained retention ([ZDNet Korea](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20230223102403)). Second, **do not pay for installs at the top of the funnel**; the entire Korean boom was word-of-mouth-led, and Colorver's CEO states its paid performance marketing was low while word-of-mouth arrivals were very high ([ZDNet Korea](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20230223102403)). Seeding is for creating the first wave of cards, not for buying users.

## Monetize only after the loop spins — and never at the reveal

The category's monetization pattern is consistent across every scaled player: a free diagnosis produces a free result, which builds a type-labeled product catalog, onto which commerce and premium services are attached. Jamface built a **124,115-item per-type product database** and a "화장품 매칭 할인" service; Colorver runs color-driven commerce plus paid offline consulting that sells out monthly at **400–500 customers**; COCON sells its diagnostic currency and planned in-app commerce ([Platum](https://platum.kr/archives/179303); [Edaily](https://www.edaily.co.kr/News/Read?newsId=01574406635604984); [ZDNet Korea](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20230223102403); [AI Times](https://www.aitimes.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=152702)). The addressable prize is not diagnosis services but Korea's roughly **₩3 trillion color-cosmetics market**, and the intent is already measurable: Olive Young's color-ready campaign lifted April color-cosmetics sales **+56% year over year** and lip makeup **+133%**, while Rom&nd's overseas sales passed **₩100 billion** on the back of personal-color-focused color cosmetics ([ZDNet Korea](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20230223102403); [KPI뉴스](https://kpinews.kr/newsView/179555630165354); [대한경제](https://www.dnews.co.kr/uhtml/view.jsp?idxno=202504141138183130252)).

| Monetization option | How it works | Evidence it fits | Paywall placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cosmetics affiliate/commerce** | Recommend specific shades in the user's palette; affiliate links and matching discounts | Jamface's 24× product saves post-diagnosis; Olive Young +56%/+133%; Rom&nd ₩100B; Jamface 124K-item DB | Behind the reveal: pay for products, never for the palette |
| **Retail / B2B embedding** | License the diagnosis into stores, kiosks, and apps; supply first-party product data | Olive Young SkinScan passed **1M cumulative diagnoses** and is rolling photo-based 컬러핏 to **100+ stores**; GS25 kiosk; Toss mini-app | Free to the consumer; the retailer pays |
| **Paid human review** | Expert or colorist verifies/refines the AI result | COCORY already pairs an AI kiosk with a human colorist; Colorver sells out 400–500 consulting slots/month | A paid upsell *after* the free reveal |
| **Premium diagnostics** | Adjacent paid tests (skin type, face shape, wardrobe audit) | Jamface's 스킨매칭 hit **1.5M uses in 3 months** across ~4,000 products | Separate product; the color reveal stays free |
| **Ads** | In-feed or reward-based advertising once scale is reached | Jamface monetizes with in-app ads and a store | Never interrupt the reveal or card |
| **Physical cards & education/IP** | Sell tone cards, kits, certification/curriculum | COCORY sells 52-card tone sets and runs an academy; certification is an exported product | High-margin back end, not a gate |

The paywall rule is absolute: **free the reveal; paywall the depth.** The type label, best/worst palettes, and share card power the loop and must stay unlocked; monetize product recommendations, deeper reports, human review, premium diagnostics, and physical cards ([App Store: Dressika](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1536745174); [App Store: My Best Colors](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/my-best-colors/id1459421275)). COCON's currency-gated diagnosis is the outlier and likely trades top-of-funnel for ARPU — acceptable only after virality is established, never as a launch strategy ([AI Times](https://www.aitimes.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=152702)).

## What kills this: fragile color science, skin-tone inequity, an unshareable card

The underlying science is the most under-appreciated risk. "Season" is a commercial convention, not a validated instrument: a peer-reviewed study of **200 Korean women** diagnosed with UK, Japanese, Korean, and German drape sets found the systems weight different axes — Korea most by **L\***, Germany by **a\*** — and that the Korean system assigned winter types the **highest b\* (yellowness)**, a result the authors note "does not follow the previous theories of personal color" ([Cha & Kim 2011](https://doi.org/10.15810/jic.2011.7.4.002)). No peer-reviewed test–retest or inter-rater reliability study of seasonal color analysis was found, and no head-to-head accuracy study of any Korean AI analyzer exists. The selfie pipeline compounds this: smartphones capture in an unknown RGB space that "prevents a standardized colorimetric skin analysis," yielding errors of **ΔE 1.8–6.6** with inter-phone variability of **ΔE 2.0–3.9**, while consumer colorimeter apps measured forehead skin at roughly **ΔE 7.6** versus a spectrophotometer — and ΔE ≈ 3–5 is already clearly visible, enough to flip a season bucket ([Cugmas 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33142901/); [Niakan 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39627966/)). Fixed-threshold ITA on uncontrolled images scored **22.9% accuracy against a 51.3% majority-class baseline** and failed on the darkest skin type, and deep-learning models underperformed a trivial constant-mean predictor on localized skin features across **3,203 consumer selfies** ([Skin Research and Technology 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42578529/); [Scientific Reports 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42570980/)). Skin tone is systematically encoded in imaging data and confounds models, so tools that work on light skin fail on deeper tones ([J Biophotonics 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41914466/)).

The reputational risk is downstream of the science. A WhatColors reviewer reported getting **six different results back-to-back with nothing changed** and concluded "this color analysis stuff is all a scam," and colorism research documents that skin-color anxiety is real and commercially exploited ([App Store: WhatColors](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/whatcolors-ai-face-try-on/id6478835459); [Frontiers in Sociology 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42729134/)). Design around all of it, specifically: **report a confidence score and nearest-neighbor types rather than one authoritative verdict**, so the output matches how science-forward Korean providers already present axes ([Δme](https://deltame.app/blog/12-season-personal-color-types)); **make the output deterministic** so repeat scans do not flip, directly fixing the complaint that fueled the "scam" review; **frame the contrast as "less flattering on you," blaming the pairing, never the person or the color**; make worst colors optional and secondary; guide capture explicitly (5000–6500K, no HDR or beauty mode, reject mixed lighting); avoid all color-psychology claims and use "styling suggestion" language; minimize retention of facial images; and make **deep-skin error a launch gate**, reporting error stratified by ITA/Fitzpatrick type, not an afterthought.

The honest and unusual conclusion is that **honesty is a growth feature, not a constraint**. The offline ritual's power comes precisely from the reveal being experiential; an app that fabricates a glow or asserts certainty the camera cannot deliver invites the exact backlash that has already produced "scam" reviews and Korean wiki-level cautions that results vary by shop and that users should not be too bound by them ([Naver search via notes](https://namu.wiki/w/%ED%8D%BC%EC%8A%A4%EB%84%90%20%EC%BB%AC%EB%9F%AC)). A transparent, uncertainty-aware reveal is both more defensible and more shareable.

**Kill criteria — what must be true for this to work:**

| Must be true | Falsifiable test | If false |
|---|---|---|
| The card actually fans out | Organic share/invite-driven new sessions exceed a set share of total new users within one quarter, with no paid UA | Change the artifact (label, contrast, or card format) before building anything else |
| One result reliably creates more than one user | Invite and friend-compare links produce a measurable new-session rate per completed result | The compatibility layer is not the wedge; test celebrity/type-compare formats instead |
| The output is stable | Repeat scans on the same photo and device do not flip the type above an acceptable threshold | Reduce claimed precision, add calibration, or pivot to a palette-first "styling" product |
| Accuracy holds across skin tones | Per-ITA error is acceptable for deeper tones, not just light skin | Do not launch globally; ship as a light-skin-only tool in the markets where it holds |
| Users come back | Day-30 retention clears a viable bar, not just cumulative reach | Virality without retention means the commerce layer has no audience to monetize |

**What the research could not settle.** No K-factor, CAC, or channel breakdown exists publicly for any personal-color app; only Colorver's qualitative "low paid, high word-of-mouth" statement is on record, and all user counts are company- or press-reported **cumulative** figures, not active or retained users ([ZDNet Korea](https://zdnet.co.kr/view/?no=20230223102403)). Platform-native analytics comparing drape-reveal versus type-reveal versus duet formats are unavailable, and **2026 hashtag volumes are unknown** — the only hard social numbers are "personal color analysis korea" at **375M TikTok views** and a creator video at **30M+**, both from 2023–2024 journalism ([Time](https://time.com/6299099/personal-color-analysis-korea-tourism/); [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/posts/from-k-pop-to-tiktok-is-color-season-analysis-a-missed-opportunity-for-brands)). There is no measured conversion from K-pop or influencer content to installs, no financials for any global incumbent, no primary source for the Japanese terminology, and no validated accuracy benchmark for any AI tool — which is precisely why the strategy must be judged on loop mechanics the team can instrument itself, not on the industry's unverifiable claims.

## Conclusion

The classifier is not the product and never was; the loop and the card are. The category's winners grew on a free, visual, self-referential diagnosis whose shareable unit is a face under contrasting colors, and the one mechanic they all left on the table is the compatibility layer that made MBTI a social operating system — color chemistry between two people, which turns every result into two users. The strategic implication is that the first engineering investment should be the cheapest possible version of the reveal on a zero-install rail, instrumented for fan-out, with the compatibility mechanic shipped early rather than the wardrobe or commerce layers. The second implication is that the "Korean authenticity" angle is a hook, not a moat: it supplies vocabulary, K-pop reach, and a travel narrative, but the defensible position is the localized, shareable, uncertainty-honest result that the incumbents — service brands and legacy taxonomy owners — have not built. Win the loop in Korea, then let the same link carry a localized card into every market where personal color is already a norm.
